## [wd-2026-11-16] - 2026-11-16

_Working Draft — November 16, 2026_

- **Semantic Form Principle** ([§3](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-16/FAF.md#3-principi)): Clarified that file and directory hierarchy and naming directly determine encapsulation boundaries and permitted dependencies in FAF.
- **Horizontal Hierarchies between Logical Domains** ([§4](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-16/FAF.md#4-fondamenti)): Generalized the exception clause to wherever the specification admits Fractal-Tree-wide Horizontal Hierarchy models.
- **Separation of Responsibilities and Role Catalog** ([§6.1.2](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-16/FAF.md#612-separazione-delle-competenze-e-univocit%C3%A0-del-symbol), [§6.1.3](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-16/FAF.md#613-horizontal-hierarchy-interna-e-modello-dei-roles), [§6.3.1](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-16/FAF.md#631-mutua-esclusivit%C3%A0-del-contenuto-e-category-nodes)): Recommended not bypassing separation of responsibilities by bundling distinct domain logic or cases into a single Symbol; refined Role catalog minimality criteria (avoiding both collapsing primary domain units into a generic auxiliary Role — obscuring their nature and preventing Fragments from composing Fragment Nodes of that Role — and introducing speculative or single-unit Roles, clarifying that Role qualifies architectural responsibility and Rank while unit-specific purpose belongs in the Fragment or Logical Node name); aligned `Category Nodes` form guidance with composition in Fragment Nodes or Sub-Fragments.
- **Self-Description by Scale Level** ([§8.1](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-16/FAF.md#81-collocazione-per-prossimit%C3%A0-e-rilevanza-di-dominio), [§6.2.3](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-16/FAF.md#623-esempi-di-riferimento)): Recommended verifying at every scale that a Logical Domain's direct children alone suffice to identify the sub-problem's units, architectural responsibilities, and dependency direction without inspecting subtrees or code — flagging concentration of primary units in a single generic auxiliary Category and generically named procedural wrapper Fragments that should be reabsorbed into the enclosing Functional domain's direct-child Logical Nodes and Private Categories; aligned Example 6.2.3.1's note.

## [wd-2026-11-15] - 2026-11-15

_Working Draft — November 15, 2026_

- **Nature of the Framework** ([§1.1](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-15/FAF.md#11-natura-del-framework)): Contrasted class-based object-oriented encapsulation and abstraction with FAF's file-and-directory modularity, favoring the functional paradigm and composition over inheritance.
- **Horizontal Hierarchies between Logical Domains** ([§4](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-15/FAF.md#4-fondamenti), [§6.3.4](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-15/FAF.md#634-horizontal-hierarchies-per-categories-e-sub-categories), [§6.4.2](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-15/FAF.md#642-horizontal-hierarchies-tra-layers)): Specified that a Horizontal Hierarchy between Logical Domains targets a single parent Logical Domain and orders direct children actually present under it across at least two distinct Ranks, permitting Fractal-Tree-wide Horizontal Hierarchy models exclusively for Sub-Categories (partitioning Sub-Category names distinct from all catalog Category names across at least two distinct Ranks) and excluding tree-wide models and Role-Rank ordering for Categories and Layers.
- **Role Reference Model and Category Forms** ([§6.1.3](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-15/FAF.md#613-horizontal-hierarchy-interna-e-modello-dei-roles), [§6.3.1](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-15/FAF.md#631-mutua-esclusivit%C3%A0-del-contenuto-e-category-nodes)): Added Role catalog design criteria (minimality, Rank ordering and alignment, and separation across contractual boundaries), recalibrated the illustrative Role Reference Model from Ranks 0–17 to Ranks 0–13 (introducing `contract`, removing `class` and `controller`, moving `constant` before `type` and `api`/`repository` to Rank 10, and updating all reference examples), and added recommendations for choosing between `Category Nodes` and `Fragments` forms.
- **Placement, Encapsulation, and Contraction** ([§6.2](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-15/FAF.md#62-root-fragments), [§8.1](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-15/FAF.md#81-collocazione-per-prossimit%C3%A0-e-rilevanza-di-dominio), [§8.2](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-15/FAF.md#82-controllo-della-profondit%C3%A0-e-dualit%C3%A0-tra-incapsulamento-e-horizontal-hierarchy), [§8.3](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-15/FAF.md#83-evoluzione-iterativa-e-ricorsiva-dellarchitettura)): Clarified recursive _Screaming Architecture_ legibility across scales and alignment between Logical Domain containment and dependency flow; articulated two progressive degrees of encapsulation (within a single Fragment's Fragment Nodes, then in the subtree via Private Categories and Fractal Branches); recommended encapsulating correlated shared responsibilities inside a Fractal Branch into a single Fragment rather than _n-tier_ sibling Categories; included reabsorbing Sub-Fragments into Parent Fragment Nodes under Contraction.

## [wd-2026-11-14] - 2026-11-14

_Working Draft — November 14, 2026_

- **Root Fragments** ([§6.2](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-14/FAF.md#62-root-fragments), [§6.3.5](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-14/FAF.md#635-mimetic-path-structure-e-path-sub-categories)): Clarified the use of Root Fragments both as external tool frontiers (including bundler entry points and file-system routing) and to surface the system's primary semantically significant functional partitions directly at the Fractal Tree root, updating Example 6.2.3.1 and contrasting with Mimetic Path Structures.
- **Placement by Proximity and Domain Relevance** ([§8.1](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-14/FAF.md#81-collocazione-per-prossimit%C3%A0-e-rilevanza-di-dominio)): Grounded unit placement in actual current consumers (excluding preemptive placement for nonexistent consumers), specified the boundary between Private Category and lowest-common-ancestor Fractal Branch across Fragment Nodes, Root Nodes, Sub-Graph Nodes, and Category Nodes, and distinguished domain semantic relevance from potential technical reusability.
- **Depth Control and Horizontal Hierarchies** ([§8.2](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-14/FAF.md#82-controllo-della-profondit%C3%A0-e-dualit%C3%A0-tra-incapsulamento-e-horizontal-hierarchy)): Established encapsulation as the default priority over Horizontal Hierarchies (restricting Horizontal Hierarchies to external exposure of dependencies and semantic prominence) and recommended not placing or promoting units into higher Fractal Branches to bypass Peer Separation between sibling units.
- **Promotion** ([§8.3](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-14/FAF.md#83-evoluzione-iterativa-e-ricorsiva-dellarchitettura)): Specified that Promotion occurs upon the actual appearance of consumers outside the current perimeter and targets the Fractal Branch of the lowest common ancestor Logical Domain of all actual consumers.

## [wd-2026-11-02] - 2026-11-02

_Working Draft — November 2, 2026_

- **Complete Specification Rewrite** ([`FAF.md`](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-02/FAF.md)): Rewrote the specification from the ground up as a formal TypeScript architectural framework:
  - Defined conformance via mandatory **norms** vs. `_è raccomandato_` **recommendations** and architect **Declarations** ([§1.2](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-02/FAF.md#12-stato-del-documento-e-conformit%C3%A0)).
  - Formalized **Fractal Trees**, the static **Dependency Graph**, **Logical Nodes** vs. **Foreign Nodes**, **Assets**, root-scope **Symbols**, **Roles** (explicit, implicit, absent) and **Execution Sub-Graphs** (`Sub-Graph Roles`, `Sub-Graph Nodes`), **Logical Domains** (`Functional` vs. `Organizational`), **Horizontal Hierarchies** (`Ranks`), and `_` / `kebab-case` naming ([§3](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-02/FAF.md#3-principi), [§4](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-02/FAF.md#4-fondamenti)).
  - Established the four **Dependency Graph Laws**: Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG), Peer Separation, Symbol Uniqueness, and No Re-Export ([§5](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-02/FAF.md#5-leggi-del-dependency-graph)).
  - Defined the complete **Logical Domain Taxonomy** (`Fragments` with `Fragment Nodes`, `Master Node`, `Access Node`, and `Sub-Graph Nodes`; `Root Fragments` and `Root Nodes`; `Categories`, `Category Nodes`, `Sub-Categories`, `Private Categories`, and `Mimetic Path Structures`; `Layers` and `Sub-Layers`; `Root Layers`; `_<scope>@shared/` `Fractal Branches`; `Foreign Domains`), the **Containment Matrix**, and **Classification Completeness** ([§6](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-02/FAF.md#6-tassonomia-dei-logical-domains)).
  - Specified **Fractal Tree Frontiers and External Relations** (`Public Surface`, inter-tree DAG dependencies, external local codebase files, and `Path Aliases` anchored to Fractal Branches) and **Design and Evolution Criteria** (`Extraction`, `Promotion`, `Contraction`, and external local dependency isolation) ([§7](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-02/FAF.md#7-frontiera-e-relazioni-esterne-dei-fractal-trees), [§8](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-02/FAF.md#8-criteri-di-progettazione-ed-evoluzione)).
- **Removed Legacy Documents**: Removed `HOW_TO_EXTEND_FAF.md` and `README_TEMPLATE.md`, superseded by formal Declarations and taxonomy rules in `FAF.md`.
- **Repository Governance and Documentation**: Rewrote [`README.md`](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-02/README.md), refined [`BIG_BALL_OF_MUD.md`](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-02/BIG_BALL_OF_MUD.md), and added [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](https://github.com/morriconeluca/fractal-architecture-framework/blob/wd-2026-11-02/CONTRIBUTING.md), `CODEOWNERS`, GitHub issue forms, and the pull request template.
